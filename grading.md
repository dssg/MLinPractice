## Assignments

All assignments are submitted on [Canvas](https://canvas.cmu.edu/courses/56403).

Two notes on how the GenAI thread shows up in these:

- **Implement Baseline(s)** includes an **LLM zero/few-shot baseline** — the
  generative baseline you either beat or beat *with*.
- **Iteration 2** is where each team **adds one component from Module 2**
  (a fairness audit, interpretability/uncertainty analysis, robustness check,
  field-evaluation design, or a foundation-model / agent component).

## How the final project is assessed

The final writeup, demo, and presentation are evaluated on:

| Criterion | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Problem framing & scoping | Is the problem well-posed and is ML the right tool? |
| Methodological soundness | Formulation, baselines (incl. LLM baseline), validation/eval design |
| Results & honest interpretation | Results vs. baselines; limitations; failure analysis |
| Engineering & reproducibility | Modular, runnable, documented |
| Communication & recommendations | Clear to a decision-maker; actionable |

**Presentations** (pitch, update, final) are assessed on clarity to a non-team
listener, honesty about limitations, technical correctness, and time management.

## Grace days

Project teams receive **3 total grace days** for project deliverables, no more
than 1 per assignment, applied greedily.

## Participation & missing days

Attendance and participation matter in this class. You may miss up to **4
sessions** and still be considered for full participation credit; beyond that,
participation credit is reduced.
