## Grading
Project-related assignments
- Project Assignments (50%)
- Project Presentations (initial pitch, update, final) (15%)

Class attendance and participation in discussions (30%)

Weekly check-in and feedback forms (5%)

## Grace days

Project teams receive **4 total grace days** for project deliverables, no more
than 1 per assignment, applied greedily.

## Participation & missing days

Attendance and participation matter in this class. You may miss up to **3
sessions** and still be considered for full participation credit; beyond that,
participation credit is reduced.
